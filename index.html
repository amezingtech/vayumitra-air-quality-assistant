    How the score works

    Formula: score = round( (US AQI × 0.30 + inhaled dose ÷ 8) × vulnerability ), capped at 0–100.

    • Inhaled dose (modelled estimate) = PM2.5 × breathing rate × duration. Breathing rates are standard planning assumptions, not measured values: rest 0.5, walk 1.1, cycling 2.2, run 3.0 m³/hour; outdoor work 1.6, school pickup 1.1.
    • Vulnerability: healthy adult ×1.0, child under 12 ×1.3, senior 65+ ×1.3, asthma/lung condition ×1.6, pregnant ×1.4. The highest selected multiplier applies.
    • Verdicts: 0–24 Go · 25–49 Go with care · 50–74 Avoid · 75–100 Skip.
    • The score is a planning aid, not medical advice. If you have a lung condition, follow your doctor's advice first.

            Data: Live air quality & geocoding: Open-Meteo Air Quality API and Geocoding API (free, no key).
